The Tuning Room

A daily practice for the ear. You listen, you write, and the room measures how precisely you heard.

  1. Listen up to three times. Getting an item right on the first listen earns a bonus.
  2. Write exactly what you hear. Contractions as contractions. Numbers as digits or words.
  3. Come back tomorrow. Every word you miss goes to your vault and returns just before you forget it.
