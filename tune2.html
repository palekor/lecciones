The Prism

A paraphrasing studio built on the six CEFR levels. You say the same idea again in new words, from one swapped word at A1 to a whole paragraph at C2, and then you record yourself and hear it against the original.

  1. Every level is a stage on the CEFR scale. A1 to C2, five levels each. Help is given at first and taken away as you grow.
  2. The room reads for meaning, not for copying. It checks every idea is still there, how much of the original you reused, and which technique you used.
  3. Your voice is part of it. Record a paraphrase and the room sets it beside the original: pace, flow and what you actually said.
