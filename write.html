The Loom

A daily writing practice. You begin by joining two phrases with one connector, and end by building a full argument with an opener, a main argument and a close.

  1. The words are given first. Every level shows the phrases you need. As you improve, they are tucked away, then removed.
  2. The room reads your writing. It checks the connector, the ideas, the structure and the punctuation, and shows exactly what earned points.
  3. A phrase is yours when you write it from memory. Three times without help, and it moves from your toolbox to your own voice.
